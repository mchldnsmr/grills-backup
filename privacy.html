Privacy
Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our site or showroom. Written in plain English so you actually know what you are agreeing to.

Last Updated June 22, 2026
Effective Date June 22, 2026

Outdoor Grill Sales is a small family business in Weatherford, Texas, owned and operated by Michael and Sandee Densmore. We are not a big corporation with a data team. We do not sell your information. We do not run ads on this site. We do not have a marketing department or a CRM full of leads.

This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. If you have any questions, call Michael directly at 817-550-6038 or email michael@outdoorgrillsales.com.

01 Information We Collect

When You Submit the Contact Form

If you fill out the contact form on our contact page, we collect whatever you provide, which may include:

  • Your full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • The type of request (showroom visit, repair, install, etc.)
  • Product category or brand you are interested in
  • Preferred date for a visit, if you provide one
  • Any additional details you choose to share in the message field

This information is submitted through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. Formspree delivers your submission to Michael's email inbox. We use only the information you choose to provide.

When You Book Through Calendly

If you book a showroom visit through the Calendly scheduling widget on our contact page, Calendly collects:

  • Your name
  • Email address (to send you the booking confirmation)
  • Your answers to a few custom questions, like how you found us and whether you are working with a contractor
  • The date and time you select

Calendly stores this information on their servers and sends Michael a notification. We can view it through Michael's Calendly dashboard.

When You Just Visit the Site

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may automatically collect standard server log information for every visitor, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages you visited and when
  • The referring URL that brought you here

This is standard for any website on the internet. We do not run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking or advertising scripts on this site. We do not set cookies. We do not retarget you with ads elsewhere on the web.

02 How We Use Your Information

Plain and simple: we use the information you give us to respond to your request. That is it.

  • To call or email you back about your showroom visit, repair, or product question
  • To prepare for your visit if you scheduled one
  • To follow up if you asked us to
  • To answer any specific questions you sent us

We do not add you to any mailing list, email blast, or marketing funnel. If we contact you, it is because you asked us to.

03 Who We Share It With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone. Ever.

The only third parties that have access to your information are the services we use to run the business:

  • Formspree processes our contact form submissions. Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Calendly handles our scheduling. Calendly's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts our website. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps embeds our location map. Google's privacy policy.

If a court or law enforcement legally requires us to share information, we will comply with the law. Otherwise, your information stays between you and us.

04 How Long We Keep It

Contact form submissions and Calendly booking records stay in Michael's inbox and account for as long as is reasonable for business purposes, generally one to three years. If you want your information removed sooner, just ask. We will delete it.

05 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Ask us to correct any information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Opt out of any future contact from us at any time

To exercise any of these rights, call Michael at 817-550-6038 or email michael@outdoorgrillsales.com. We will respond as quickly as we reasonably can, usually the same business day.

If you are a Texas resident, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) may give you additional rights. We honor all valid Texas resident privacy requests.

06 Children's Privacy

Our site and business are intended for adults shopping for grills and outdoor kitchens. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through our site, please contact us and we will delete it.

07 Security

We use reputable third-party services (Formspree, Calendly, Cloudflare) that handle data security on their platforms. We never store payment information on our site. We do not process credit cards or financial transactions online. Any payments for grills, repairs, or installations are handled in person at the showroom or through Michael directly.

That said, no method of transmitting information over the internet is 100 percent secure. We do our best to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and update the "Last Updated" date at the top. Material changes will be reflected here. If you want to know what changed, just ask.
