On-Site Grill Repair

Yes. All of our grill repair is done on site at your home. Michael drives out, diagnoses the problem in place, and repairs it right there in your outdoor kitchen. This is how we work with every repair customer, because most of the grills we service are built into an island and cannot be moved.

Yes. Built-in grills are serviced in place. There is no need to pull the unit out of the island, disconnect it, or haul it anywhere. Built-in owners are exactly who our on-site repair service is designed for.

We repair gas grills and pellet grills. Common jobs include igniters that stopped clicking, burners heating unevenly, regulator and valve problems, worn cooking grates and flavorizer bars, lid and hinge repairs, and full cleaning and performance tune-ups.

Yes. It does not matter where you bought the grill or who installed it. We service grills regardless of where they came from, including outdoor kitchens that were built by another company.

Replacement & Outdoor Kitchens

Yes, and this is one of the main things we do. We remove the old unit, confirm the new grill fits the existing opening, and set it in properly. If the cutout does not match a current model, we will tell you what your options are before you commit to anything.

Yes. We design and build full custom outdoor kitchens, from a single built-in grill in an island to a complete setup with refrigeration, storage, side burners, and heating. We also coordinate gas line hookup as part of the install.

Yes. You do not have to hire us to install anything. We sell every piece of equipment an outdoor kitchen needs, whether we are doing the installation or your own builder, landscaper, or pool contractor is. Send your contractor to us and Michael will help spec the equipment so it fits the plan the first time.
