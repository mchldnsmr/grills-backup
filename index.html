Our Story

Meet Michael
& Sandee

Est. 2017  |  Weatherford, TX
Now at 111 Sunray Ct
"What started as a side job became our calling. We built Outdoor Grill Sales on one belief: take care of the customer first, every single time."

Michael and Sandee Densmore opened Outdoor Grill Sales in Weatherford, TX in 2017. What began as a side hustle fueled by a passion for great barbecue grew into a full-time family business. Today they carry premium grill brands, handle on-site repairs, and build and outfit complete outdoor kitchens.

When you buy a grill from Outdoor Grill Sales, you are not just buying a product. You are getting Michael and Sandee in your corner. And when something needs hands-on work, Michael comes to you.
