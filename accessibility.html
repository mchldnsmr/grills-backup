Accessibility
Statement

Everyone should be able to find a great grill. Here is our commitment to keeping this website usable for all visitors, and how to tell us if something is not working for you.

Last Updated June 22, 2026
Effective Date June 22, 2026

Outdoor Grill Sales is committed to making our website usable for as many people as possible, including visitors who use assistive technology such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or screen magnification. We believe everyone deserves the same friendly, no-pressure experience online that they would get walking into our showroom.

We are a small family business, not a big company with a dedicated web team, but accessibility matters to us and we take it seriously. If anything on this site is hard to use, we want to hear about it so we can fix it.

01 Our Commitment

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level as our standard. These guidelines are the widely accepted benchmark for making web content more accessible to people with a range of disabilities, including visual, hearing, motor, and cognitive differences. We treat accessibility as an ongoing effort, not a one-time checkbox.

02 What We Have Done

In building and maintaining this site, we have worked to include features that support accessibility, such as:

  • Descriptive text alternatives for images so screen readers can describe them
  • Clear, consistent navigation across every page
  • A layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktop screens
  • Readable font sizes and color contrast intended to support legibility
  • Labeled form fields on our contact form
  • Keyboard-accessible menus and links

03 Ongoing Effort and Known Limitations

Accessibility is something we keep working on. Some parts of the site rely on third-party tools and content that we do not fully control, and these may not always meet the same standard. This includes:

  • Manufacturer catalogs and brochures (PDFs) produced by the brands we carry
  • The embedded Google Maps location map
  • The Calendly booking widget used to schedule showroom visits
  • The Shopify-powered online store, which is operated on Shopify's platform

If you run into trouble with any of these, please reach out and we will do our best to help you directly, by phone or in person, so you still get what you need.

04 We Are Here to Help In Person

If any part of this website is difficult for you to use, you never have to navigate it alone. Michael is happy to walk you through brands, pricing, promotions, repairs, or scheduling directly. Call 817-550-6038 and we will give you the same information you would find on the site, with a real person on the line.

05 Report an Accessibility Issue

If you come across a page, feature, or piece of content on this site that is not accessible to you, please let us know. Tell us what happened, which page you were on, and what would have made it easier, and we will work to fix it. Your feedback genuinely helps us make the site better for everyone.
