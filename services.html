Service 01  ·  On-Site & Honest

On-Site Grill
Repair

If your grill is built into an island, you cannot exactly unbolt it and haul it across town. So we come to you. Michael drives out to your home, diagnoses the problem in place, and fixes it right there in your outdoor kitchen.

We now offer repair service for gas grills and pellet grills alike. Igniter stopped clicking, burners heating unevenly, regulator giving out, controller acting up: tell us what it is doing and we will get you back to grilling. It does not matter where you bought it or who installed it.

  • Built-in grills serviced in place, no removal needed
  • Gas grill repair
  • Pellet grill repair
  • Igniter and burner replacement
  • Regulator and valve issues
  • Grate and flavorizer bar replacement
  • Lid and hinge repairs
  • Cleaning and performance tune-ups

Service 02  ·  Out With the Old

Equipment Removal
& Installation

Do you have an older built-in grill or other equipment that is past its prime? Is it time to have it removed and replaced, but you do not know where to start? That is exactly what we do. We pull the old unit out, make sure the new one fits the opening, and set it in properly.

It does not matter who built your outdoor kitchen. If someone else put it in years ago and the equipment is failing, call us. We will come look at what you have, tell you honestly what fits, and handle the swap start to finish.

Starting from scratch instead? We still design and build complete custom outdoor kitchens, from a single built-in grill to the full setup.

  • Old built-in grill removal and haul-off
  • New built-in grill installation
  • Retrofit sizing for existing cutouts and openings
  • Side burner and power burner replacement
  • Griddle and pizza oven swaps
  • Access door, drawer, and component replacement
  • Full custom outdoor kitchen builds
  • Gas line hookup coordination
